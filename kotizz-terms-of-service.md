### 2.1 Organizers
An Organizer is the person who creates a sòl group. Organizers authenticate using Sign in with Apple, or Magic Link (email) if not on an iOS device. The Organizer is responsible for:
- Setting up the group's terms (amount, frequency, payout order, number of members);

Participants do not create accounts.

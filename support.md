## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I create a sòl group?**
Open Kotizz, tap "Create Group," fill in your group name, contribution amount, and payment schedule. You'll automatically become the group organizer.

**How do participants join?**
Share your group's invitation link via WhatsApp, Facebook, or any messaging app. Participants don't need to create an account — they just follow the link to view and join.

**How does ID verification work?**
Participants upload a photo ID when joining. As the organizer, you review and approve each request from your dashboard before they're added to the group.

**Do participants need to download the app?**
No. Participants can view their group and payment status through a secure link, without creating an account.

**What's included in the free plan?**
The free plan includes 1 active group with up to 5 members. Upgrade to Kotizz Pro for unlimited groups and members.
